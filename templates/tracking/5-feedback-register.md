**How This Connects to Wave Learning Logs:**
- **Wave Learning Logs** capture what happened in each Wave (W-001, W-002, etc.)
- **This Project Register** analyzes patterns across all Waves
- **Individual Feedback** (FD-YYYY-XXX) provide detailed issue tracking
- **Together** they create a complete learning system for continuous improvement

**Quick Start Guide:**
1. **Complete 2+ Waves** with their Wave Learning Logs first
2. **Look across Waves** - What issues appear in multiple Waves?
3. **Identify trends** - Are things getting better or worse over time?
4. **Plan systematic changes** - Address root causes, not just symptoms
5. **Update every 2-3 Waves** to track progress and adjust plans

**This Register Helps You:**
- See the forest, not just the trees (big picture vs individual Wave issues)
- Prevent the same problems from happening again and again
- Track whether your process improvements actually work
- Plan better for future projects based on real experience

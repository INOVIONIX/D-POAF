# Project AI Tracker

## Overview

This template helps you analyze AI collaboration patterns across multiple Waves. Think of it as "building your AI knowledge base" - collecting successful prompts, identifying trends, and improving team AI skills over time.

**Use this when:** You've completed 2+ Waves using AI and want to understand what's working consistently across your project.

- Team learning progression
- ROI of AI collaboration

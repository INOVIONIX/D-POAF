**Use this when:** You're about to start a new Wave and want to make sure your team and AI tools have all the context they need to work effectively.

**Quick Start Guide:**
1. **Fill out the goal** - Be specific about what this Wave will accomplish
2. **Gather context** - Collect all the background information the team needs
3. **Prepare AI context** - Write clear descriptions your AI tools can understand
4. **Align the team** - Make sure everyone understands their role
5. **Get approval** - Confirm everyone's ready to start building

**Tips for Better Wave Setup:**
- Be specific about goals - vague goals lead to vague results
- Include examples - help your team (and AI) understand patterns
- Gather resources upfront - don't hunt for information mid-Wave
- Plan for questions - have clear escalation paths
- Keep it accessible - everyone should understand the context

**This Setup Enables:**
- More effective AI prompts (AI understands your context)
- Faster team onboarding (all info is accessible)
- Better decisions during building (clear goals and constraints)
- Smoother Wave execution (everyone aligned from the start)

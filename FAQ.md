# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### What is D-POAF?
D-POAF is a complete ecosystem for verifiable software delivery, consisting of a methodology (ready now) and a platform (in development).

### Can I use D-POAF now?
Yes! The methodology is complete and can be implemented manually. Download the 130-page guide and start today.

### When will the platform be ready?
MVP targeted for Q2 2026. Timeline depends on community contributions.

## ⚡ How to use (5 minutes)

1. **Duplicate** the template (link above)
2. **Read** the 📖 Practical Guide (15 min)
3. **Adopt** the ⚖ Dynamic Laws with your team (review the 15 rules)
4. **Open your first Wave** in 🌊 Waves (delete the example or keep it as reference)
5. **Log every AI invocation** in 🤖 Prompt Actions
6. **Close each Wave** with a ✅ Proof Record

That's it. You have governance from day 1.

## 🔗 The traceability chain

The killer feature: from any commit hash or PA-ID, walk back to business intent in 4 clicks (~30 seconds):

```
1. Code commit [AI:claude:PA-001]
       ↓ Ctrl+P in Notion → "PA-001"
2. PA page (Model + Config + Context = HOW)
       ↓ Click "Wave" relation
3. Wave page (Business Objective = WHY)
       ↓ Click "Related Proofs"
4. Proof Record (PoD/PoV/PoR = DID IT WORK?)
       ↓ Audit conclusion
```

The full procedure is documented in the **🔗 Traceability** page of the template.
